# Initial Data Inspection

I began by loading the supplied CSV dataset into Jupyter Notebook.

My initial inspection identified:
- One missing price.
- One exact duplicate row.
- An inconsistent product label: `oil b` instead of `Oil B`.
- A suspicious price of 9999.
- Price values that needed conversion into numeric format.
